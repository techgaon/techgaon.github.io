Panchayat AI — Delete your account and data

App: Panchayat AI (in.techgaon.panchayatai) · Developer: TechGaon AI Services

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to techgaonai@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Panchayat AI account".
  2. Send it from the Google email you use to sign in to Panchayat AI (or mention that email in the message).
  3. If you only want some data removed (for example saved drafts or chat history) and want to keep the account, write "Delete my Panchayat AI data" and say what should be removed.

We confirm the request by replying to that email. We never ask for your password or OTP.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (name, email, photo, mobile number, role, district/block/Gram Panchayat, letterhead details)
  • Saved letters, notices, drafts, AI questions and history
  • Your sign-in record in Firebase Authentication

What may be kept

Payment/invoice records for plans bought on our website may be kept as required by tax law (up to 8 years). Backups are removed within 30 days.

Time

Deletion is completed within 30 days of the request.
